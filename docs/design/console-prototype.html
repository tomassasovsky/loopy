Cards are placements

Each card is a rack, on something, at a stage — the owner is the headline, the rack name is the accent line. Vacant slots are dashed.

Printed or not

The chip under the tabs says whether that stage is recorded into the take or monitor-only. Input is the only one that prints.

The open question

In Pedal, targets are named racks. Slots live under Show individual effects. Is one tap deeper the right price for a short list?

Monitor states

Open a rack in FX. auto is the default — heard while its track is armed or selected.

Names are yours

Double-click a track to rename it; open an FX card to rename its input. The hardware says input 2; you say mic.
